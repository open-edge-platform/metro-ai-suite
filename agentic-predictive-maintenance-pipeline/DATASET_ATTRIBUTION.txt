4. Gas Pipeline Defect Detection

"Gas pipelines" by Sardaana Eginova, published on Kaggle at kaggle.com/datasets/sardaanaeginova/gas-pipelines. The publisher declares this dataset to be made available under the MIT License. The dataset comprises photographic imagery of undamaged and damaged gas pipes located in Almaty, Kazakhstan, annotated for corrosion, deformation and coating degradation. It contains 8,448 images with annotations in COCO Segmentation format, exported via Roboflow on 12 January 2025. Images were auto-oriented and resized to 640x640; no augmentation was applied.
